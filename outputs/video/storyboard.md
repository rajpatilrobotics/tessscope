Editorial rules:

- Never imply that the cached replay is live inference.
- Never describe PQ as percent accuracy.
- Keep the exact-vs-stopped confidence interval and exact-vs-piecewise failure together.
- Keep “validation-only,” “sealed test,” and “no physical microscope” visible in the close.
- No synthetic microscopy, AI-generated scientific imagery, music, or third-party voice.
